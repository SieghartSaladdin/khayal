# Code of Conduct

Khayal is a small open source project. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, share work, and disagree about design without it getting personal.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Criticize ideas, work, and results, not people.
- Be specific and constructive. "This reference is outdated, here is a newer one" helps. "This is garbage" does not.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming someone made a mistake on purpose.
- Respect different levels of experience, languages, and backgrounds. Many contributors write in a second language.
- Give credit. Say where an idea, a source, or a piece of text came from.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or submitting content you know is false, such as made-up sources, awards, or benchmark numbers.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where people represent or talk about the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, tell the maintainers privately. Open the maintainer's profile on GitHub (the account that owns this repository) and use the contact method listed there, or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. Please include a link and a short description. Reports are treated seriously and kept confidential as far as possible.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who repeatedly or seriously break this code. We will try to explain the decision.

This code of conduct is written in our own words, with the goals of widely used community codes in mind.
